About Me

As Senior IT Manager at Rex-Tone Industries Ltd., I lead strategic technology initiatives focused on infrastructure modernization, ERP implementation, cybersecurity governance, and operational excellence. My role centers on aligning technology investments with business objectives by delivering secure, scalable, and efficient solutions that enhance organizational performance and support sustainable growth. I oversee enterprise IT operations, network and server infrastructure, information security programs, vendor management, and digital transformation initiatives, ensuring technology remains a key enabler of business success. A significant area of my expertise is leveraging Microsoft Power BI as the organization's advanced business analytics and decision-support platform. I design and implement end-to-end data solutions by connecting, transforming, and modeling data from multiple business systems, including ERP, finance, operations, procurement, and manufacturing environments. Through the development of interactive dashboards, automated reporting frameworks, KPI scorecards, and real-time analytics, I enable leadership teams to make informed, data-driven decisions with greater speed and accuracy. By transforming complex datasets into actionable business intelligence, Power BI has become a critical tool for improving operational visibility, performance monitoring, strategic planning, and continuous process improvement across the organization. My professional focus combines technology leadership, cybersecurity, enterprise systems management, business intelligence, and process automation. I am passionate about utilizing emerging technologies, cloud platforms, analytics, AI-driven automation, and data visualization to simplify business processes, improve operational efficiency, strengthen security posture, and create measurable business value. Through a balanced approach to innovation, governance, and execution, I help drive digital transformation initiatives that enhance competitiveness and support long-term organizational success.
